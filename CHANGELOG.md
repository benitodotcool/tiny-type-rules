## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-05

### Added

- French and English rules: spaces before punctuation, quotes and apostrophes, ellipsis, thousands separators, number and unit, abbreviations.
- Opt-in rules: dashes, last-word widows, literal replacements.
- `fixText`, `fixParts`, `fixHtml`, `fixPortableText`, `fixElement` and the `tiny-type-rules/rehype` plugin.
- `createTypo` to override any setting per language, define regional variants and add languages.
- HTML scoping through `lang`, `data-ttr-lang`, `data-prevent-ttr` and `data-ttr`.
